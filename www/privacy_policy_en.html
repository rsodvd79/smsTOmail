Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how data is collected, used and disclosed when you use the "SMS to MAIL" application.

App purpose and use of the SMS permission

The main and sole function of "SMS to MAIL" is cross-device SMS transfer/synchronization: the app receives incoming traditional (GSM) SMS on your phone and forwards them by email, letting you read and archive them from another device (PC, tablet, another phone) through your mailbox. The Android RECEIVE_SMS permission is requested and used exclusively for this purpose and is never used for any purpose other than the one described here.

Information collected

Our application collects the following information:

  • SMS messages: The app accesses incoming SMS messages on your device solely to forward them by email. Messages are processed locally on the device and are never uploaded to third-party servers, sold, or shared for profit.
  • Email configuration: We collect the information needed to configure forwarding to your email account. In SMTP mode, credentials (password) are encrypted (AES-256/GCM via Android Keystore) and stored exclusively on your device. In Gmail API mode, no password is required: the app uses an OAuth 2.0 token obtained through the Google account you choose, with the sole permission to send email (gmail.send scope), managed by Google's authentication services and never visible or accessible to the developer.
  • Filters: Any filtering rules (sender/keyword) set up to decide which SMS to forward are stored locally on the device.

Use of data

We use the collected data exclusively to provide the application's services, specifically to:

  • Forward received SMS messages to the specified email address
  • Apply filters to messages to be forwarded
  • Ensure the correct operation of the application

Data is not used for marketing purposes or shared with third parties.

Data storage and security

All collected data is stored locally on the user's device. Email login credentials are protected through encryption.

We do not transfer data to our own servers, except for sending messages via the SMTP protocol to the email servers specified by the user.

Network access

The application requires Internet access solely to send emails. We do not collect browsing data or other information related to network usage.

User rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Delete the application's data from your device settings
  • Uninstall the application at any time
  • Modify or delete saved email configurations
  • Modify message filters

Children's data

Our application is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
