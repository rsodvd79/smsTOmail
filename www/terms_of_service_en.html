1. Description of the service

"SMS to MAIL" is an Android application that intercepts traditional (GSM) SMS messages received on the user's device and automatically forwards them by email to an address configured by the user, for the purpose of synchronizing one's own messages across multiple personal devices (phone, computer, tablet). The App does not provide any messaging relay, third-party server backup, or retrieval of authentication/OTP codes on behalf of third parties.

2. Requirements and permissions

To function correctly, the App requires the following system permissions, explicitly granted by the user at startup:

  • RECEIVE_SMS — to intercept incoming SMS and forward them by email;
  • INTERNET — to send emails via SMTP or the Gmail API;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — to show notifications when sending fails.

The user may revoke these permissions at any time from the device settings; doing so will cause the App to stop working or work only partially.
