TEXT TO ENTER IN THE FIELD "Explain how your app uses the APIs/permissions
for sensitive information access"
(Play Console limit: 500 characters - the text below is 453 characters):

---

smsTOmail intercepts SMS received on the Android device and forwards them in
real time via email to the address the user configured, letting them read
messages on other devices (PC, tablet, another phone) linked to that
mailbox. RECEIVE_SMS is needed to intercept incoming SMS: without it this
cross-device forwarding/sync, the app's sole purpose, would not be possible.
The app does not read SMS history and does not send messages on the user's
behalf.
